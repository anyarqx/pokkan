You will make a short announcement. You only need a browser for this step.

## Create the Kit

<img src="/screenshots/pokkan-create-message.webp" alt="New Kit form with a name and Legacy or Components V2 format choices" width="2560" height="1552" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

1. Open [Messages](https://pokkan.net/en/kits).
2. Give your Kit a name, such as **Weekly announcement**, or leave the field blank to use the suggested name. This name identifies your saved work in Pokkan and stays separate from the text sent to Discord.
3. Choose **Legacy** for this first message. It supports ordinary message text, embeds and polls.
4. Choose **Create message**.

## Edit the message

Click or tap the message text in the preview and replace it with your announcement. Click outside the text to finish editing. If you change the wrong thing, use **Undo** below the preview.

:::note[Check your result]
Your own text appears in the editor. Nothing has been posted to Discord yet.
:::

For a Components V2 message, click its Text Display to write in the same way. The next guide introduces the other elements available in that format.

Your draft is kept in this browser. Do not clear browser storage if you want to keep an unsaved draft.
