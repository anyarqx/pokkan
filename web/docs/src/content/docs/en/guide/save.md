title: Save and reopen a Kit
description: Understand local drafts, account saves and portable Pokkan Kit backups.

Save a Kit to your account to reopen it and continue editing in another browser or on another device. Without signing in, you can keep working on a local draft in this browser.

1. Give the Kit a recognizable name.
2. If you want account storage, sign in with Discord using the account control in the topbar.
3. Make a change and choose **Save** in the editor.
4. Open the Messages page or sidebar and select the name to reopen it.

Without signing in, editing still keeps a draft in this browser. A local draft is not a cloud backup. Clearing site data or moving to another browser can make it unavailable.

**Discard** returns to the saved version when one exists. For a new, unsaved Kit it removes the draft. Download **Pokkan Payload** through JSON before discarding work you may want later.

Open the Kit's **…** menu for renaming, duplication or deletion, or use right click as a shortcut. You can also click the title in the editor or press F2 on the Kit's sidebar link to rename it inline. Enter accepts the new name and Escape cancels it. Save the renamed draft to keep the change in your account. Deleting asks for confirmation and removes the Kit from Pokkan without deleting any message already sent to Discord.

To save a server Kit, choose your server before creating it. The private collection requires server ownership, Administrator or Manage Server permission, even when opening a Kit by its URL. Existing Kits do not move when you change the selector. [Account and servers](/en/using-pokkan/servers/) walks through installation and selection.

If another person saved first, Pokkan keeps your draft and reports a conflict. **Keep a copy and load latest** saves your pending work as a separate local draft before opening their version. [Kits and drafts](/en/using-pokkan/kits/) explains how to return to that copy.

Confirm saving by reopening the Kit after the success notice. If Save fails, keep the draft and download a backup instead of refreshing immediately.
