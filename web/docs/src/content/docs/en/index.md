Pokkan lets you build Discord messages by editing their preview. Start with ordinary text, an embed, or a Components V2 layout. You can try the editor without signing in or installing a bot.

**New here? [Follow the beginner guide →](/en/guide/start/)**

Already working on a message? [Open Pokkan](https://pokkan.net/en/kits), [create a webhook](/en/guide/create-webhook/), or [solve a sending problem](/en/using-pokkan/troubleshooting/).

## Learn by doing

1. [Create your first message](/en/guide/first-message/).
2. [Learn the editor controls](/en/guide/editor/).
3. [Add an embed](/en/guide/embed/), or [try Components V2](/en/guide/components-v2/).
4. [Send it to Discord](/en/guide/send/).
5. [Save and reopen your work](/en/guide/save/).

## Continue with a real project

- [Select, move and edit parts](/en/using-pokkan/editing/): direct controls, insertion, mobile and keyboard use.
- [Kits, drafts and renaming](/en/using-pokkan/kits/): save safely and resolve conflicting changes.
- [Account and servers](/en/using-pokkan/servers/): installation, ownership and channel/role references.
- [Find and fix errors](/en/using-pokkan/validation/): follow diagnostics before exporting or sending.

The beginner Guide is available in English, Português (Brasil), Español and 日本語. The detailed element and control references are available in English and Portuguese, with clearly marked English fallback pages where a translation is not yet available.
